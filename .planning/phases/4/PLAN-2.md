# Phase 4: Replies after quiet - Plan 2: The page reads the one record, and a reply waits for him

## Goal

Land `stream/replies-after-quiet` on one record of when Void is making sound, shared by the native `Speaker` and the page's player, in place of the separate guesses. This plan does the page half: the page reports its own sound, reads "is he making sound" from the server, and holds a follow-up until he has finished.

## Must be true when done

- The page reports through `/api/speaking` each sentence it is handed (an owe), and each sentence it schedules (with `left`) or finds has no audio (a pay). The server's record covers both
- `app/static/index.html` has one "is he making sound" test, `sounding()`. It reads the newest copy of the server's record (sound ahead, or a sentence owed) and the page's own unanswered hand-off posts, nothing else. `untilQuiet`, `whenQuiet`, `followTry`, `heldTry` and `quietNative` all wait on it, and none waits on `playing`, `q.length` or `spkMap.size`
- A follow-up kept while he works or speaks leaves his line playing to its last chunk, and is sent exactly once, after the line ends. That includes a follow-up kept in the pause between two of his sentences while the next one is still being made. No follow-up shushes him
- "stop" or "Void" said over him still stops his playback before the line ends
- Both accepted coverage commands exit 0 on the final tree, and the phase's diff touches no dev-tooling path. Merging `macos` into `personal` gives no conflicts, and nothing is pushed

- Runs after Plan 1, in `/Users/atom/dev/void-quiet` on `macos`. It reads the event Plan 1 Task 7 named (look it up in `app/server.py`), which carries `left`, `owed` and `n`. The replies to `/api/say`, `/api/say/stop` and `/api/speaking` carry the same fields.
- Locked: D-01 (the page's hold gets "is he making sound" from the server rather than from its own `playing`/`q.length`/`spkMap` state, one test on the page, no placeholder span), D-10 (the page owes and pays each sentence, and `sounding()` reads sound ahead, `owed > 0` or `handing > 0`), D-11 (no start-bound: `untilQuiet` and `whenQuiet` wait only while `sounding()`), D-03 (a follow-up kept while he speaks or works is held until he has finished speaking, sent once, never shushes him; only a stop phrase or "Void" cuts him off), D-05 (land on `macos`, merge into `personal`), D-08 (page tests on installed Chrome).
