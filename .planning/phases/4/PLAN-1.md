# Phase 4: Replies after quiet - Plan 1: One record of his sound, echo only while it was in the air

## Goal

Land `stream/replies-after-quiet` on one record of when Void is making sound, shared by the native `Speaker` and the page's player, in place of the separate guesses. This plan does the server half: the record, the gate's `rang`, the replay, the report the page will read, and the regression rows.

## Must be true when done

- Sound written through the native `Speaker` and sound the page reports through `/api/speaking` both make `Wake.air` report talking over that time. The pause after a sentence counts as sound only when his next sentence is already made, and `/api/speaking` records no placeholder span
- A reply begun after his sound has ended, the ring included, is classed `follow` (or `wake` once the window has closed) and never `over` or "while talking"
- Through the real hearing path, his own line heard back while it plays is dropped as echo. The same line heard 0.5 s after his sound ends is dropped as echo, and the same text heard 1.5 s after is kept
- `void drops` replays an echo drop whose own heard line shows it clear of him as kept, and prints "as logged" for an echo drop the log cannot clear
- Every `tests/test_log_cases.py` row whose reason named Phase 4 passes with no mark, or keeps a strict mark whose reason the phase SUMMARY states
- `Wake.owed` counts every sentence handed to a player whose sound has not begun. The native `Speaker` and the page's `/api/speaking` posts add and pay it, a skipped sentence pays, and a cut-off clears it. `Wake.air` and the gate read none of it
- The server tells the page, over `/api/events` and in the replies to `/api/say`, `/api/say/stop` and `/api/speaking`, how long his sound in `Wake.talked` still runs, what he still owes, and a sequence number that marks the newest copy
