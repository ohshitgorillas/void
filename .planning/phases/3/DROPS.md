# Phase 3: Real-log replay - Reported drops (Plan 1)

The owner's reported drops, mined from `~/.local/share/void/server.log` (198000 lines, stamped 26 Oct 20:30 to 5 Nov 21:26, older lines unstamped) for Plan 2's regression table. Plan 2 reads this file and does not write it.

How to read it:

- Heard texts are quoted as logged (the log keeps at most 80 characters). A row classed echo shows what the mic heard, which is Void's own voice heard back; the lines Void spoke, as `history.jsonl` records them, are never quoted. Evidence about what Void had said is in plain words of my own, because this file lives on `personal` and Phase 3 D-02's privacy line is kept here too.
- Only the owner's short request texts may reach `tests/` (D-02). Nothing here is test data until Plan 2 chooses it.
- Log line numbers are 1-based in `server.log`. A row with two lines is one burst judged twice (the quick listen for his name, then the full burst: the same words a few lines apart with a `heard` line between); its verdict and `talking` are the later (full) judgement's.
- "Today's verdict" is what `app/drops.py` `now()` returns for the row: `kept`, `kept: stops him`, the drop reason, or `as logged` (the log does not hold what echo, no speech or low confidence need).
- `talking` is the `while talking` flag of the `heard` line before the verdict line (the later line of a pair).
- Classes: **owner** (his words), **room** (not a request to Void: noise, other voices, or his own aside to the room), **echo** (Void's own voice heard back), **borderline** (the log cannot settle who spoke; the owner rules in the Borderline section). `Not his` holds room and echo rows, including correct drops that a signal named.
- Phases: 4 = a reply after Void went quiet or while he talks, dropped as echo, while talking or no wake word (HEAR-01, HEAR-02); 5 = stop phrases (HEAR-03, HEAR-04); 6 = music or other voices in the follow-up window (HEAR-06); none = today's verdict already equals the expected one.
- The expected verdict is a `gate.WHY` value. "kept" names the act in brackets: follow for a follow-up, request for a woken request.

## Method

Everything was mined by throwaway scripts in the session scratchpad (none committed); the log is read locally and never copied into `tests/`. Every verdict, kept or dropped, is one log line; a pre-stamp line is read through `drops.py`'s old-wording table, and `follow-up request`, `wake word request`, `barge-in: you told him to stop` and `voiceprint enrolling ... of your speech` count as kept lines (enrolment is speech the old server accepted as his). Kind of a pre-stamp drop and its `talking` come from the `heard` line before it. The log holds 128472 verdict lines, 126723 of them drops.

**Task 1 phrases.** A drop of kind `follow`, `over` or `wake` whose text, lower-cased with punctuation dropped, holds "its good", "yes do it", "yes god damn" or "yes goddamn". `peek` hits are left out unless a seed or a named example (line 36695).

**Signal 1, repeated after a drop.** A drop of kind `follow`, `over` or `wake`, text not empty and not `gate.is_filler`, followed by a kept burst (any kept line except `peek`) holding the same words: one text inside the other (the shorter at least two words), equal, or a difflib ratio of 0.8 or more on the lower-cased, punctuation-free texts. The kept burst must come within 60 s on stamped lines, or within 40 log lines on pre-stamp lines. A repeat of the wake kind may come from the quick listen of the same burst, so a kept burst in the same second is marked "nothing lost".

**Signal 2, he complained.** A `you` entry of `history.jsonl` or a kept log text containing "dropping", "dropped", "didn't hear", "not listening", "are you listening", "can you hear", "I said" or "I just said" (apostrophes ignored). History entries that open with a date stamp and a bracketed tag are batched pings from other Claude sessions, not his speech, and are left out (two). That gave 13 complaints. Candidates are the drops of the same kinds and text rule in the 3 minutes before it (the 100 log lines before it on pre-stamp lines; on stamped lines, only drops stamped before the complaint's own line).

**Rule for a doubled verdict.** The quick listen and the full burst of one utterance are one row (two lines).

**Classification.** owner: a seed or `YOURS` text, or the same words were kept again (signal 1), or a request whose wording is his in the log's neighbours. echo: the gate dropped it as echo and it is a 5-word or longer stretch, or a run of 12.0 s bursts, that he did not repeat; or the heard text is a loop of Void's voice. room: a sound (Ahh), a phrase repeated in a loop, a stock line from video, or talk plainly with someone else; his own asides to the screen with no request are room too (correct drops). borderline: anything else; each carries my lean. A 12 s cap burst is a burst at the length limit (continuous sound), which is how Void reading aloud looks in the log.

**Evidence available.** `history.jsonl` runs from 30 Oct 23:31 to 5 Nov 17:48, so it cannot date pre-stamp lines nor any line stamped 26 to 30 Oct before 23:31. Those rows rest on the log's own neighbours: kept requests and complaints near them, bursts flagged while talking, and the length and word count of the heard burst. Rows after that point were checked against the history rows around them (for example line 95890, where his next request is in `history.jsonl`).

**Counts printed by the mining script.** 33 signal-1 hits and 63 signal-2 hits among 12343 eligible drops; 85 candidates not already a Task 1 row.
